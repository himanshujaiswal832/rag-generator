# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA)

**Effective Date:** January 15, 2026  
**Provider:** CloudScale Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation  
**Customer:** Global Enterprise Solutions Ltd.  

---

### SECTION 4. FEES, INVOICING, AND PAYMENT TERMS
4.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay all subscription fees, usage charges, and professional services fees set forth in the applicable Order Form.
4.2 **Payment Terms.** All invoices are due and payable Net-30 days from the invoice date in United States Dollars (USD). Undisputed overdue amounts shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.
4.3 **Taxes.** All fees are exclusive of sales, use, excise, VAT, or other governmental taxes, which Customer is solely responsible for remitting.

---

### SECTION 7. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & UPTIME COMMITMENT
7.1 **Monthly Availability Commitment.** Provider commits to delivering a Monthly Uptime Percentage of not less than **99.95%** across all production enterprise tenants, calculated calendar month by month, excluding Excused Downtime (scheduled maintenance not exceeding 4 hours per calendar month announced 5 business days in advance).
7.2 **Service Credits.** If the Monthly Uptime falls below 99.95%, Customer is entitled to receive Service Credits as follows:
- **99.0% to < 99.95%**: 10% credit of the monthly prorated subscription fee.
- **95.0% to < 99.0%**: 25% credit of the monthly prorated subscription fee.
- **< 95.0%**: 50% credit of the monthly prorated subscription fee.
7.3 **Sole Remedy.** Service Credits are Customer's sole and exclusive financial remedy for any unavailability or performance degradation of the CloudScale platform.

---

### SECTION 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
11.1 **Consequential Damages Waiver.** NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, PUNITIVE, OR EXEMPLARY DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, DATA LOSS, BUSINESS INTERRUPTION, OR REPUTATIONAL DAMAGE.
11.2 **Aggregate Liability Cap.** EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF SECTION 9 (CONFIDENTIALITY), SECTION 12 (INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS), OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE / WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE STRICTLY LIMITED TO AND SHALL NOT EXCEED **$5,000,000 (FIVE MILLION USD)** OR THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE INCIDENT, WHICHEVER IS GREATER.

---

### SECTION 14. TERM AND TERMINATION
14.1 **Initial Term.** The initial term of this Agreement commences on the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for three (3) consecutive years unless terminated earlier.
14.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon providing at least **sixty (60) days prior written notice** to the other party.
14.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either party may terminate immediately if the other party breaches any material term and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days of receiving written notice specifying the default.
