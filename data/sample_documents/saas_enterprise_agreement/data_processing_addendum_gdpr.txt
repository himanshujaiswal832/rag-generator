DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM (GDPR & CCPA COMPLIANCE)

1. RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES
Customer acts as Data Controller and CloudScale Technologies acts as Data Processor regarding Personal Data processed under the Agreement.

2. SECURITY INCIDENT NOTIFICATION
CloudScale shall notify Customer without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of any confirmed Personal Data Breach impacting Customer Data. 
The notification shall describe:
(a) The nature of the security incident, categories of personal data, and approximate number of data subjects impacted.
(b) Likely consequences of the personal data breach.
(c) Mitigation measures already executed or proposed to be taken by CloudScale.

3. SUB-PROCESSORS
Customer provides general written authorization for CloudScale to engage infrastructure sub-processors (including AWS and Cloudflare).
CloudScale shall provide Customer with at least thirty (30) days advance written notice prior to authorizing any new sub-processor.
Customer may object in writing on reasonable data protection grounds within fourteen (14) days of notice receipt.

4. AUDIT RIGHTS
CloudScale shall make available to Customer an annual SOC 2 Type II audit report and ISO 27001 summary certification to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 of the GDPR.
Upon thirty (30) days advance notice and subject to reasonable confidentiality procedures, Customer or an independent third-party auditor may conduct an on-site audit once every twelve (12) calendar months.
