# CLINICAL PROTOCOL: STUDY TX-409-201

**Title:** A Phase II, Multicenter, Open-Label Trial of TX-409 in Patients with Advanced or Metastatic Claudin-18.2-Positive Gastric and Gastroesophageal Junction (GEJ) Adenocarcinoma.  
**Investigational Product:** TX-409 (Recombinant Humanized IgG1 Monoclonal Antibody).  
**IND Number:** 148,920  
**Phase:** Phase IIa/IIb  

---

### 1. DOSAGE AND ADMINISTRATION
1.1 **Standard Dosage:** TX-409 is administered as an intravenous (IV) infusion at a dose of **8.0 mg/kg** every three weeks (Q3W, 21-day cycles).
1.2 **Infusion Duration:** The first infusion must be administered over 90 minutes (±10 minutes). Subsequent infusions in patients without infusion-related reactions (IRRs) may be shortened to 60 minutes.
1.3 **Premedication:** Mandatory premedication 30 to 60 minutes prior to each infusion:
- Diphenhydramine 50 mg IV or oral equivalent
- Acetaminophen 650 mg to 1000 mg oral
- Dexamethasone 8 mg IV (for Cycles 1 and 2 only)

---

### 2. PRIMARY AND SECONDARY ENDPOINTS
2.1 **Primary Efficacy Endpoints:**
- **Objective Response Rate (ORR):** Proportion of patients achieving confirmed Complete Response (CR) or Partial Response (PR) assessed via RECIST v1.1 by independent central review committee (ICRC).
- **Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** Time from the date of first dose until objective radiographic disease progression or death from any cause, with targeted median PFS of not less than 8.4 months.

2.2 **Secondary Endpoints:**
- **Overall Survival (OS):** Median duration of survival from cycle 1 day 1.
- **Disease Control Rate (DCR):** CR + PR + Stable Disease (SD) maintained for >= 12 weeks.
- **Duration of Response (DoR)** and safety/tolerability incidence rates.

---

### 3. STATISTICAL DESIGN AND SAMPLE SIZE
A total of **140 evaluable patients** will be enrolled across 24 investigational sites in North America, Europe, and East Asia. An interim futility analysis will occur after the first 45 patients complete at least two post-baseline tumor assessments (Week 12).
